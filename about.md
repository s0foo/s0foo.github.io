# About Me

I'm a dad, a tech enthusiast, and a lifelong learner based in Paris. I love
building clean and efficient things that make life easier, more secure, and
more enjoyable - whether that's through code, design, or just a well-organized
to-do list.

I live in **Paris with my wife and our daughter** enjoying the chaos and charm

never dull. I currently work in Paris, but I'm also **open to opportunities in

team in a dynamic city like London excites me - it's all about the next
adventure!
